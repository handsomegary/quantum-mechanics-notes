\notepage{58}{Spin and the product Hilbert space}
For a spin-$s$ system, the magnetic quantum number takes the values
\[
 m_s=-s,-s+1,\ldots,s-1,s,
\]
giving $2s+1$ spin states, with $S_z$ eigenvalues $m_s\hbar$.
The spatial Hilbert space and spin Hilbert space are separate factors.
For a product state, write
\[
 \ket{\Psi}=\ket{\psi}\otimes\ket{\chi},
\]
where $\ket{\psi}$ is spatial and $\ket{\chi}$ carries the spin.

\smallheading{A discrete spatial basis}
For spatial basis vectors $\ket{x_1},\ldots,\ket{x_N}$, the product
basis coefficient is
\[
 (\bra{x_a}\otimes\bra{m_s})(\ket{\psi}\otimes\ket{\chi})
 =\braket{x_a}{\psi}\braket{m_s}{\chi}.
\]
Ordering the coefficients first by spin label gives the column
\[
 \begin{bmatrix}
  \braket{x_1}{\psi}\braket{s}{\chi}\\
  \vdots\\
  \braket{x_N}{\psi}\braket{s}{\chi}\\[2pt]
  \braket{x_1}{\psi}\braket{s-1}{\chi}\\
  \vdots\\
  \braket{x_N}{\psi}\braket{-s}{\chi}
 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
\smallheading{A continuous spatial representation}
On replacing the discrete position labels by a continuous $x$, the
same state is represented by a $(2s+1)$-component wavefunction,
\[
 \boldsymbol\Psi(x)=
 \begin{bmatrix}
  \braket{x}{\psi}\braket{s}{\chi}\\
  \braket{x}{\psi}\braket{s-1}{\chi}\\
  \vdots\\
  \braket{x}{\psi}\braket{-s}{\chi}
 \end{bmatrix},\qquad
 \Psi_{m_s}(x)=\psi(x)\chi_{m_s}.
\]
The source also writes the product as $\psi(x)\chi_m$.
\editorial{The factorized expression describes a product state.
A general spin-position state may require independent component
functions $\Psi_{m_s}(x)$ and need not factor into one common
$\psi(x)$ times a spin vector.}
