\notepage{45}{Angular-momentum eigenstates and ladder operators}
Within a fixed energy subspace, suppress the energy label and write
\[
 L^2\ket{\alpha,\beta}=\alpha\ket{\alpha,\beta},
 \qquad L_z\ket{\alpha,\beta}=\beta\ket{\alpha,\beta}.
\]
Define $L_\pm=L_x\pm\ii L_y$, so $L_+^\dagger=L_-$.
Because $L^2=L_x^2+L_y^2+L_z^2$,
\begin{align*}
 L_-L_+&=(L_x-\ii L_y)(L_x+\ii L_y)
 =L_x^2+L_y^2+\ii\comm{L_x}{L_y}\\
 &=L^2-L_z^2-\hbar L_z,\\
 L_+L_-&=L^2-L_z^2+\hbar L_z.
\end{align*}
In particular,
\[
 L_-L_+\ket{\alpha,\beta}
 =(\alpha-\beta^2-\hbar\beta)\ket{\alpha,\beta}.
\]
\editorial{Here $\alpha$ is the eigenvalue of $L^2$, not its square
root. Thus the intermediate $\alpha^2$ in the handwriting is replaced
by $\alpha$.}

The commutators are
\[
 \comm{L^2}{L_\pm}=0,\qquad
 \comm{L_z}{L_\pm}
 =\comm{L_z}{L_x}\pm\ii\comm{L_z}{L_y}
 =\pm\hbar L_\pm.
\]
As in the harmonic-oscillator ladder construction,
\begin{align*}
 L_zL_+\ket{\alpha,\beta}
 &=\bigl(L_+L_z+\comm{L_z}{L_+}\bigr)\ket{\alpha,\beta}\\
 &=(\beta+\hbar)L_+\ket{\alpha,\beta},\\
 L^2L_+\ket{\alpha,\beta}
 &=L_+L^2\ket{\alpha,\beta}
 =\alpha L_+\ket{\alpha,\beta}.
\end{align*}
Choosing a single irreducible ladder, with any additional labels
suppressed, therefore gives
\[
 L_+\ket{\alpha,\beta}=C_+^{\alpha\beta}
 \ket{\alpha,\beta+\hbar},\qquad
 L_-\ket{\alpha,\beta}=C_-^{\alpha\beta}
 \ket{\alpha,\beta-\hbar}.
\]
The zero vector is allowed at either endpoint of the ladder.
