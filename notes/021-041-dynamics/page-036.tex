\notepage{36}{Nondegeneracy of One-Dimensional Bound States}
\smallheading{Theorem}
There is no degeneracy among bound states of the ordinary
one-dimensional Schr\"odinger problem on a connected line, with a real
scalar potential and the usual bound-state boundary conditions.

\smallheading{Proof exercise}
The source assigns the proof as homework and supplies no proof on this page.

\editorial{The qualifications specify the standard setting of the
handwritten theorem. The statement is not intended to cover disconnected
domains, internal degrees of freedom, or arbitrary boundary conditions.}
