\notepage{31}{Parity Eigenstates of the Free Particle}
Since $\Pi^\dagger P^2\Pi=P^2$, we have $\comm\Pi{P^2}=0$ and, for
the free-particle Hamiltonian, $\comm\Pi H=0$.
For a fixed $E>0$, use $\ket r=\ket{|p|}$ and $\ket l=\ket{-|p|}$.
The parity matrix in this two-direction basis is
\[
 \Pi\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \begin{bmatrix}
 \mel r\Pi r&\mel r\Pi l\\
 \mel l\Pi r&\mel l\Pi l
 \end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}
 \braket rl&\braket rr\\
 \braket ll&\braket lr
 \end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Its eigenvalues and normalized coefficient vectors are
\[
 +1:\quad\frac{\ee^{\ii\alpha}}{\sqrt2}\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},
 \qquad
 -1:\quad\frac{\ee^{\ii\beta}}{\sqrt2}\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Thus
\[
 \ket+=\frac{\ee^{\ii\alpha}}{\sqrt2}
       \bigl(\ket{|p|}+\ket{-|p|}\bigr),\qquad
 \ket-=\frac{\ee^{\ii\beta}}{\sqrt2}
       \bigl(\ket{|p|}-\ket{-|p|}\bigr).
\]
These are generalized eigenstates; the two-component normalization here
is the relative normalization of the two channels at fixed $|p|$.
\smallheading{Position-space wavefunctions}
Using the plane-wave convention of note 26,
\begin{align*}
 \braket x+
 &=\frac{\ee^{\ii\alpha}}{\sqrt2}
       \bigl(\braket x{|p|}+\braket x{-|p|}\bigr)\\
 &=\frac{\ee^{\ii\alpha}}{\sqrt{4\pi\hbar}}
       \bigl(\ee^{\ii|p|x/\hbar}+\ee^{-\ii|p|x/\hbar}\bigr)
 =\frac{\ee^{\ii\alpha}}{\sqrt{\pi\hbar}}\cos\frac{|p|x}{\hbar},\\
 \braket x-
 &=\frac{\ee^{\ii\beta}}{\sqrt2}
       \bigl(\braket x{|p|}-\braket x{-|p|}\bigr)\\
 &=\frac{\ii\ee^{\ii\beta}}{\sqrt{\pi\hbar}}\sin\frac{|p|x}{\hbar}.
\end{align*}
A convenient real-wavefunction phase convention is
$\alpha=0$, $\beta=3\pi/2$.
