\notepage{24}{Momentum as the Generator of Translations}
Starting from $T^\dagger XT=X+\epsilon I$ and using unitarity,
\[
 XT=TX+\epsilon T=TX+\epsilon I+O(\epsilon^2).
\]
Substitution of $T=I-\ii\epsilon G/\hbar+O(\epsilon^2)$ gives
\[
 XT-TX=-\frac{\ii\epsilon}{\hbar}\comm{X}{G}
 =\epsilon I+O(\epsilon^2),\qquad \comm{X}{G}=\ii\hbar I.
\]
Likewise, $T^\dagger PT=P$ implies $\comm{P}{G}=0$.
Identifying the translation generator with momentum gives
\[
 G=P,\qquad \boxed{\comm{X}{P}=\ii\hbar I.}
\]
\editorial{The source identifies $G$ with $P$ by the usual momentum and
phase convention for spatial translations. The two commutators alone
do not fix an additive scalar multiple of the identity.}
\smallheading{Translation of a wavefunction}
Write $\psi(x)=\braket{x}{\psi}$ and use $T(\epsilon)\ket{x}=\ket{x+\epsilon}$:
\begin{align*}
 T(\epsilon)\ket{\psi}
 &=T(\epsilon)\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ket{x}\braket{x}{\psi}\,\dd x\\
 &=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ket{x+\epsilon}\braket{x}{\psi}\,\dd x\\
 &=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\ket{x'}\braket{x'-\epsilon}{\psi}\,\dd x'.
\end{align*}
Therefore
\[
 \mel{x}{T(\epsilon)}{\psi}
 =\int\underbrace{\braket{x}{x'}}_{\delta(x-x')}
       \braket{x'-\epsilon}{\psi}\,\dd x'
 =\psi(x-\epsilon).
\]
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=Stealth,x=1cm,y=0.65cm]
 \draw[->] (-2,0)--(3.4,0) node[right] {$x$};
 \draw[ink,thick,domain=-1.9:2,samples=60] plot (\x,{exp(-2*\x*\x)});
 \draw[dashed,thick,domain=-0.9:3,samples=60] plot (\x,{exp(-2*(\x-1)*(\x-1))});
 \draw[->] (0,1.35)--(1,1.35) node[midway,above] {$\epsilon$};
 \node at (-1.3,1) {$\psi(x)$};\node at (2.4,1) {$\psi(x-\epsilon)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
Expanding both sides to first order,
\[
 \psi(x)-\frac{\ii\epsilon}{\hbar}\mel{x}{P}{\psi}
 =\psi(x)-\epsilon\frac{\dd\psi}{\dd x}+O(\epsilon^2),
 \qquad\boxed{\mel{x}{P}{\psi}=-\ii\hbar\frac{\dd}{\dd x}\braket{x}{\psi}.}
\]
