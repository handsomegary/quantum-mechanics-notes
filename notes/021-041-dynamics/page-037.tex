\notepage{37}{The Harmonic Oscillator: Factorization}
The energy eigenvalue problem is
\[
 \left(\frac{P^2}{2m}+\frac12m\omega^2X^2\right)\ket E=E\ket E.
\]
Classically,
\[
 \mathcal H=\frac{p^2+m^2\omega^2x^2}{2m}
 =\frac1{2m}(m\omega x-\ii p)(m\omega x+\ii p).
\]
Quantum mechanically the commutator changes this factorization:
\begin{align*}
 \frac1{2m}(m\omega X-\ii P)(m\omega X+\ii P)
 &=\frac1{2m}\left[m^2\omega^2X^2+P^2
                  +\ii m\omega(XP-PX)\right]\\
 &=H-\frac{\hbar\omega}{2},
 \qquad \comm XP=\ii\hbar I.
\end{align*}
Introduce
\[
 \alpha=\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}},\qquad
 \beta=\sqrt{\frac1{2m\omega\hbar}},\qquad
 \alpha\beta=\frac1{2\hbar}.
\]
The factored expression can also be written
\begin{align*}
 H-\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}
 &=\hbar\omega\left[\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}X^2
       +\frac1{2m\omega\hbar}P^2+\frac{\ii}{2\hbar}(XP-PX)\right]\\
 &=\hbar\omega
 \left(\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}X
       -\ii\sqrt{\frac1{2m\omega\hbar}}P\right)
 \left(\sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}X
       +\ii\sqrt{\frac1{2m\omega\hbar}}P\right)\\
 &=\hbar\omega(\alpha X-\ii\beta P)(\alpha X+\ii\beta P).
\end{align*}
Thus
\[
 \boxed{H=\hbar\omega\left[(\alpha X-\ii\beta P)
                  (\alpha X+\ii\beta P)+\frac12\right].}
\]
The commutator of the two factors is
\begin{align*}
 \comm{\alpha X+\ii\beta P}{\alpha X-\ii\beta P}
 &=\comm{\alpha X+\ii\beta P}{\alpha X}
   +\comm{\alpha X+\ii\beta P}{-\ii\beta P}\\
 &=\ii\alpha\beta\comm PX-\ii\alpha\beta\comm XP\\
 &=2\hbar\alpha\beta\,I=I.
\end{align*}
