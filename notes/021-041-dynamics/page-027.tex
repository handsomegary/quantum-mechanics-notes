\notepage{27}{The Time-Evolution Operator}
The Schr\"odinger equation and the definition of $U(t)$ are
\[
 \ii\hbar\frac{\dd}{\dd t}\ket{\psi(t)}=H\ket{\psi(t)},\qquad
 \ket{\psi(t)}=U(t)\ket{\psi(0)}.
\]
First assume a time-independent Hamiltonian with a nondegenerate discrete
energy basis:
\[
 H\ket E=E\ket E,\qquad\sum_E\ket E\bra E=I,\qquad
 \ket{\psi(t)}=\sum_E\ket E\braket E{\psi(t)}=\sum_Ea_E(t)\ket E.
\]
Substitution yields
\begin{align*}
 0&=\Bigl(\ii\hbar\frac{\dd}{\dd t}-H\Bigr)\ket{\psi(t)}\\
 &=\sum_E\Bigl(\ii\hbar\frac{\dd}{\dd t}-H\Bigr)a_E(t)\ket E\\
 &=\sum_E\bigl(\ii\hbar\dot a_E-Ea_E\bigr)\ket E.
\end{align*}
Independence of the basis vectors gives
\[
 \ii\hbar\dot a_E=Ea_E,\qquad
 a_E(t)=a_E(0)\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar},\qquad
 \braket E{\psi(t)}=\braket E{\psi(0)}\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar}.
\]
Consequently,
\begin{align*}
 \ket{\psi(t)}&=\sum_E\ket E\braket E{\psi(0)}\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar},\\
 U(t)&=\sum_E\ket E\bra E\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar}.
\end{align*}
For degeneracy label $\alpha$, replace this by
\[
 U(t)=\sum_{E,\alpha}\ket{E,\alpha}\bra{E,\alpha}\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar}.
\]
A continuous part of the spectrum requires the corresponding integral
over $E$, with its proper spectral normalization.
\smallheading{Stationary states}
An energy eigenstate acquires only a phase,
$\ket{E(t)}=\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar}\ket E$. Thus a measurement in a fixed
observable basis has time-independent probabilities:
\[
 \abs{\braket{\omega}{E(t)}}^2
 =\abs{\braket{\omega}{E}\ee^{-\ii Et/\hbar}}^2
 =\abs{\braket{\omega}{E}}^2.
\]
