\notepage{33}{Free-Particle Motion and Wavepacket Moments}
Expectation values can be computed without first finding the evolving state:
\[
 \mel{\psi(t)}\Omega{\psi(t)}
 =\mel{\psi(0)}{\Omega_H(t)}{\psi(0)}.
\]
For a free particle, the Heisenberg equations are
\begin{align*}
 \frac{\dd P(t)}{\dd t}&=\frac1{\ii\hbar}
       \comm{P(t)}{P(t)^2/(2m)}=0,\\
 \frac{\dd X(t)}{\dd t}&=\frac1{\ii\hbar}
       \comm{X(t)}{P(t)^2/(2m)}=\frac{P(t)}m.
\end{align*}
Equal-time commutators are invariant under the unitary change of picture:
\begin{align*}
 \comm{X(t)}{P(t)}
 &=U^\dagger XUU^\dagger PU-U^\dagger PUU^\dagger XU\\
 &=U^\dagger\comm XP U=\ii\hbar I.
\end{align*}
Also $\comm X{P^2}=\comm XP P+P\comm XP=2\ii\hbar P$,
and more generally $\comm X{f(P)}=\ii\hbar f'(P)$.
Integration yields
\begin{align*}
 P(t)&=P(0),&P(t)^2&=P(0)^2,\\
 X(t)&=X(0)+\frac{t}{m}P(0),\\
 X(t)^2&=X(0)^2+\frac{t}{m}\bigl[X(0)P(0)+P(0)X(0)\bigr]
                  +\frac{t^2}{m^2}P(0)^2.
\end{align*}
Therefore
\[
 \langle P(t)\rangle=\langle P(0)\rangle,\qquad
 \langle X(t)\rangle=\langle X(0)\rangle+\frac{\langle P(0)\rangle}{m}t.
\]
For a momentum-labelled energy eigenstate, $\langle P\rangle=\pm\sqrt{2mE}$
formally; normalizable wavepackets have ordinary finite moments.
\smallheading{Exercise retained from the source}
Start from the unnormalized Gaussian wavepacket
\[
 \braket x{\psi_u}=\ee^{\ii p_0x/\hbar}\ee^{-x^2/(2\Delta^2)},\qquad
 \ket\psi=\frac{\ket{\psi_u}}{\sqrt{\braket{\psi_u}{\psi_u}}}.
\]
Calculate $\langle X(0)\rangle$, $\langle X(0)^2\rangle$,
$\langle P(0)\rangle$, and $\langle P(0)^2\rangle$, then use the operator
solutions to find their time-dependent counterparts and
\[
 \Delta X(t)\Delta P(t)=
 \sqrt{\langle X(t)^2\rangle-\langle X(t)\rangle^2}
 \sqrt{\langle P(t)^2\rangle-\langle P(t)\rangle^2}.
\]
\editorial{The handwritten cross term $2X(0)P(0)t/m$ in $X(t)^2$
is replaced by the symmetrized product because $X(0)$ and $P(0)$ do not commute.}
