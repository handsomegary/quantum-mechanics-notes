\notepage{75}{Choosing a basis in the degenerate subspace}
Let $D$ be the eigenspace of $H^0$ with energy $E_n^0$. Choose an
orthonormal basis $\{\ket{\bar n^0}\}$ that diagonalizes $H'$ within $D$:
\[
 \mel{\bar m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}
 =\mel{\bar n^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}\,\delta_{mn}
 \quad\text{for }E_m^0=E_n^0.
\]
The first-order equation is still
\[
 H^0\ket{n^1}+H'\ket{\bar n^0}
 =E_n^0\ket{n^1}+E_n^1\ket{\bar n^0}.
\]
For $E_m^0\ne E_n^0$, projection onto $\bra{m^0}$ yields
\begin{align*}
 E_m^0\braket{m^0}{n^1}+\mel{m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}
 &=E_n^0\braket{m^0}{n^1},\\
 \braket{m^0}{n^1}
 &=\frac{\mel{m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}}{E_n^0-E_m^0}.
\end{align*}
Thus
\[
 \ket{n^1}=
 \underbrace{\sum_{E_m^0\ne E_n^0}
 \frac{\ket{m^0}\mel{m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}}{E_n^0-E_m^0}}
 _{\ket{n^1_\perp}\in D^\perp}
 +\underbrace{\ket{n^1_\parallel}}_{\in D}.
\]
For a different basis vector in $D$, the projected equation instead reads
\[
 (E_n^0-E_m^0)\braket{\bar m^0}{n^1}
 =\mel{\bar m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0},\qquad m\ne n,
\]
which reduces to $0=0$ after diagonalizing $H'$ in $D$. This includes the
zero added as an annotation on the source page.

Normalization fixes
$\operatorname{Re}\braket{\bar n^0}{n^1_\parallel}=0$. Its component
along $\ket{\bar n^0}$ is therefore $\ii\alpha\ket{\bar n^0}$, with real
$\alpha$, and can be removed by $\ket n\mapsto\ee^{-\ii\alpha}\ket n$.
The part determined by the first-order equation is
\[
 \ket n=\ket{\bar n^0}
 +\sum_{E_m^0\ne E_n^0}
 \frac{\ket{m^0}\mel{m^0}{H'}{\bar n^0}}{E_n^0-E_m^0}
 +\ket{\eta_D}+O((H')^2),
\]
where $\ket{\eta_D}\in D$ is orthogonal to $\ket{\bar n^0}$.
\editorial{The source drops the entire within-subspace correction using
an overall-phase argument. Only the parallel component along the chosen
state can be removed that way. Mixing with other vectors in $D$ is not
fixed by this first-order equation; it generally requires higher-order
equations. The source's final sum correctly gives the correction outside
the degenerate subspace.}
