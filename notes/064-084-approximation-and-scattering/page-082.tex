\notepage{82}{Time-dependent Green's functions}
This page uses units $\hbar=1$. The free and full wave equations are
\[
 (\ii\partial_t-H_0)\psi^0(\mathbf r,t)=0,\qquad
 (\ii\partial_t-H_0-V)\psi(\mathbf r,t)=0.
\]
Their Green's functions satisfy
\begin{align*}
 (\ii\partial_t-H_0)G_0(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
   &=\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\delta(t-t'),\\
 (\ii\partial_t-H_0-V)G(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
   &=\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\delta(t-t').
\end{align*}
With matching boundary conditions, the two useful integral forms are
\begin{align*}
 \psi(\mathbf r,t)&=\psi^0(\mathbf r,t)+
 \int\dd^3r'\dd t'\,G_0(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
 V(\mathbf r')\psi(\mathbf r',t'),\\
 \psi(\mathbf r,t)&=\psi^0(\mathbf r,t)+
 \int\dd^3r'\dd t'\,G(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')
 V(\mathbf r')\psi^0(\mathbf r',t').
\end{align*}
\smallheading{Operator form and boundary conditions}
For a time-independent full Hamiltonian $H$, put
\[
 U(\mathbf r t,\mathbf r't')=
 \mel{\mathbf r}{\ee^{-\ii H(t-t')}}{\mathbf r'}.
\]
The retarded and advanced functions are
\[
 G^R=-\ii\Theta(t-t')U,\qquad
 G^A=+\ii\Theta(t'-t)U.
\]
Each satisfies the unit-delta equation, with a different time boundary
condition. The piecewise expression written in the source is
\[
 K=G^R+G^A=
 \begin{cases}
 -\ii U,&t>t',\\
 +\ii U,&t<t'.
 \end{cases}
\]
\editorial{With the delta-function normalization stated above,
$(\ii\partial_t-H)K=2\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\mathbf r')\delta(t-t')$,
not a unit delta. Thus the source's unrestricted claim that
$\alpha G^R+\beta G^A$ is a Green's function needs the condition
$\alpha+\beta=1$. In particular, the normalized symmetric choice is
$\tfrac12(G^R+G^A)$. This follows directly from the jump at $t=t'$.}
