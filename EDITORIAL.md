## Selected primary references used for checking

The first-order treatment of a degenerate subspace was checked against Barton Zwiebach's [MIT 8.06 perturbation-theory notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-06-quantum-physics-iii-spring-2018/a0889c5ca8a479c3e56c544d646fb770_MIT8_06S18ch1.pdf), particularly the distinction between selecting a basis within the subspace and determining corrections outside it.

For the convention `(Laplacian + k^2) G = delta`, the sign and inverse-distance normalization of the outgoing Helmholtz kernel were checked against the University of Manchester's [quantum-scattering notes](https://oer.physics.manchester.ac.uk/AM/Notes/jsmath/Notesse11.html). The doubled-source observation follows directly by applying the linear differential operator to the sum.

These references support selected editorial checks. They are not the source scans and are not bundled with this archive.
